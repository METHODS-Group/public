About the interview

  • Part 1: Research presentation. You will give your talk on Zoom at brown.zoom.us/my/brendankeith. Each slot is 45 minutes. Prepare a 30-minute research presentation: a five-minute overview of your collective works, followed by 20 minutes on a topic of your choice. Please consider devoting the last five minutes to highlighting connections with the group’s research on finite element methods, variational inequalities, and optimization. The rest of the slot is for questions.
  • Part 2: Individual interview. On the morning after your presentation (Thursday, December 10 or 17), you will meet with Brendan on Zoom for 30 minutes, between 10:00 am and 12:00 pm ET, to discuss your research background and to ask any questions about the position. Brendan will email you the exact time and the Zoom link. Members of the group may also reach out to you to arrange individual meetings.
  • Audience. The presentations take place during the METHODS Group meeting. They are attended by the group and open to the public, and you are welcome to attend each other’s talks.
  • Timing. Each of you has a specific time slot for both parts of the interview. Please be on time for both, and be respectful of each other’s time so that the schedule does not get delayed.

How to change your schedule
  • Find your talk below. It shows your name and GitHub login.
  • To change the title or abstract, press Edit my sign-up on your talk.
  • To move to another slot, press Cancel my sign-up on your talk, then press Sign up on an open slot.
  • Each button opens a pre-filled GitHub form. Submit it, and this page updates within about a minute; press Refresh to see the change.
  • After sign-ups for your day close (noon ET the day before), email Brendan instead.

Your name, GitHub login, title, and abstract are stored in this public repository and are visible to anyone, so enter them as you would like them shown. If you would rather not sign up through GitHub, or something goes wrong, email Brendan.
