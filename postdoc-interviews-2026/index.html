About the interview

  • Part 1: Research presentation. You will give your talk on Zoom at brown.zoom.us/my/brendankeith. Each slot is 45 minutes. Prepare a 30-minute research presentation: a five-minute overview of your collective works, followed by 20 minutes on a topic of your choice. Please consider devoting the last five minutes to highlighting connections with the group’s research on finite element methods, variational inequalities, and optimization. The rest of the slot is for questions.
  • Part 2: Individual interview. On the morning after your presentation (Thursday, December 10 or 17, Eastern Time), you will meet with Brendan on Zoom to discuss your research background, and you will have the opportunity to ask any questions about the position. Brendan will email you the time and the Zoom link. Members of the group may also reach out to you to arrange individual meetings.
  • Audience. The presentations take place during the METHODS Group meeting. They are attended by the group and open to the public, and you are welcome to attend each other’s talks.
  • Timing. Each of you has a specific time slot for both parts of the interview. Please be on time for both, and be respectful of each other’s time so that the schedule does not get delayed.

Your name, GitHub login, title, and abstract are stored in this public repository and are visible to anyone, so enter them as you would like them shown. To change your title or abstract, use Edit my sign-up; to switch slots, use Cancel my sign-up and then sign up for another open slot. If you would rather not sign up through GitHub, or something goes wrong, email Brendan.
